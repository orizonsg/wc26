Orizon (ORI) is an algorithmic DeFAI (Decentralised Finance + AI) protocol built on BNB Smart Chain by developers from OlympusDAO. It integrates AI product revenue with DeFi staking mechanics, distributing yield to stakers through automatic rebases every 3 hours 36 minutes.
Orizon Fixed Stake currently offers approximately 1,246% APY. This compounds through ~6.67 rebases per day, with each rebase adding ~0.1038% to your balance. APY is dynamic and self-regulates based on market conditions.
Fixed Stake locks your ORI for a period (90, 180, or 360 days) in exchange for significantly higher APY. Flexi Stake allows you to unstake your principal at any time, with compounding interest and lower but still competitive returns. Most long-term holders choose Fixed Stake to maximise compounding.
