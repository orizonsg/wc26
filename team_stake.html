Yield Formula
Daily Yield = 0.8 × Rebase × (M / TotalCV)

M (Weight) = Qmax + ΣQi + B × Qmax

Qi = Qi_raw × decay_i    decay_i = 0.9 ^ (level − 1)

B (Balance Factor) = min(ΣQi / Qmax, Bmax)

N (Weight) = K / Qmax       K = ORI_price × Qmax

TotalCV = Sum of all addresses' contribution values on that day.
