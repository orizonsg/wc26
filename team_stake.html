Daily Contribution Value Yield
Yield=80%× Daily Rebase Output ×N×M/Total Network Contribution Value
Equivalent form: Yield = 0.8 × Rebase × N × M / TotalCV
Weight Coefficient N
N = 0.00001 × K + 0.9

K = USD value of ORI staked
K = staked ORI amount × live ORI price
When K ≥ $10,000 → N = 1.0 (capped)
Contribution Value Weight M
M = Qmax + ΣQi + B × Qmax

B = ( ΣQi / Qmax )0.33 — capped at 1.0

Qi = member power × 0.9level−1
Level 1 = direct referral, Level 2 = one hop below, etc.

K = USD value of ORI staked and does not change unless your staked ORI amount or ORI price changes
Member power = stake base + interest earned
Qmax
Qmax = your current sORI balance

This means: Qmax = stake base + interest earned

It is your current computing power, not only your original stake base.
The same idea is used for team members' estimated computing power.
Practical Estimation Method
Because team balances grow daily from rebases and different members may sit in different tiers, the practical approach is:

1. Record each member's last known current balance from a screenshot
2. Record the date of that screenshot
3. Assign the correct tier: 360 Day, 180 Day, 90 Day, or Flexi
4. Let the tool project forward roughly until your next update

When you receive a new screenshot, update the member and the estimate re-anchors from that new date.
